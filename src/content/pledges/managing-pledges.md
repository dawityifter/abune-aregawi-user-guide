The **Treasurer → Pledges** tab shows the [Pledge Dashboard](#/pledges/pledge-dashboard) at the top and, below it, every pledge in the **live** drive. Clicking a group on the dashboard, such as an unpaid group under **Needs attention**, filters the list. **Clear filter** shows all again. If no drive is live, it says “No pledge drive is running right now”.

## The pledge list

Each row shows **Donor**, **Pledged**, **Paid**, **Outstanding** and **Status**, with **Edit** and **Cancel pledge** actions. The heading shows the count, for example “84 pledges”.

## Recording a pledge

:::admin
**Record Pledge** appears only for **Admin** and **Treasurer**. Other finance roles can see the list.
:::

Members usually pledge themselves on the **Make a Pledge** page. Record one yourself when a member pledges on paper or by phone.

:::steps
1. **Pledges → Record Pledge**.
2. Choose the member.
3. Enter the **Pledge amount** and optional **Notes**.
4. Click **Save pledge**.
:::

If the member already has a pledge in this drive, the form tells you: “{name} already has a pledge of {pledged} in this drive — {remaining} still outstanding.” Edit that pledge instead of creating a second one.

:::warning One pledge per member per drive
Two pledges for the same person in the same drive double their pledged amount and leave one of them looking unpaid. This has happened after duplicate member records were merged. See [Duplicate records](#/members/duplicate-and-incorrect-records).
:::

## Recording a pledge together with a payment

When someone pays and pledges at the same time, for example hands over $200 toward a new $500 pledge, you don’t need two steps. In **Add Payment**, Zelle Review, Square Review or Bank Reconciliation, choose payment type **Pledge Drive**. If the member has no open pledge, tick **Also record this as a pledge** and enter the pledge amount. See [How payments credit pledges](#/pledges/payments-and-pledges).

## Changing a pledge amount

:::steps
1. Click **Edit** on the pledge.
2. Enter the **New amount** and click **Save amount**.
:::

## Cancelling a pledge

:::steps
1. Click **Cancel pledge**.
2. Enter a **Reason**, for example “Duplicate of pledge recorded on paper”.
3. Click **Confirm cancel**.
:::

A cancelled pledge stays in history and no longer counts toward the drive’s totals. Use this for duplicates and withdrawn pledges. Payments already made are not deleted.

## Who still owes?

- On **Pledges**, look at the **Outstanding** column.
- On the [Pledge Dashboard](#/pledges/pledge-dashboard), **Still owed** and **Needs attention** show the overall picture.
- To remind people, use **Communications (SMS) → Pending Pledges**. It texts everyone with an outstanding balance in the live drive, with their own amount filled in. See [SMS](#/administration/sms-communications).

## Anonymous pledges and gifts

- A gift from someone who is not a member can still be a pledge drive gift. In Add Payment choose **Anonymous / Non-Member Payment** and payment type **Pledge Drive**. You can record a pledge in the donor’s name, with an optional baptism name.
- Anonymous Benevity pledge gifts are recorded as anonymous pledges named “Benevity &lt;Transaction ID&gt;”, so the treasurer can always trace them.
- The Pledge Dashboard counts these separately: “plus {count} anonymous gifts”.
