The **Employees** and **Vendors** tabs on the Treasurer Dashboard keep the lists used as **payees** in Add Expense. Keeping them tidy makes the Expense Report readable.

## Employees (“Employee Management”)

Manage church employees and staff: **Add Employee**, then search by name, email or position, and filter by **Employment Type** (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Volunteer) and **Status** (Active/Inactive). The list shows name, position, salary and pay frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, annual, per-service), with **Edit** and **Delete**.

## Vendors (“Vendor Management”)

Manage vendors and suppliers: **Add Vendor**, search by name, contact or account number, and filter by **Vendor Type** (Utility, Supplier, Service Provider, Contractor, Lender, Other) and **Status**. Columns: **Vendor Name**, **Type**, **Contact**, **Account Number**, **Payment Terms**, with **Edit** and **Delete**.

:::tip Make people inactive instead of deleting them
Only **active** employees and vendors appear in Add Expense (“No active employees found…”). When someone leaves, set them to **Inactive**. Their past expenses keep their name, and they no longer clutter the list.
:::

:::warning Sensitive information
Salaries and vendor account numbers are confidential. Only finance roles can see these tabs. Don’t print or share these lists.
:::
