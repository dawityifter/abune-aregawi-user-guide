The church application is the website the parish uses to keep its **membership list**, record **every dollar given or spent**, run **pledge drives**, and keep members informed. It replaced paper registers and spreadsheets, so the treasurer, the secretary and leadership all work from the same records.

Members see a simple website. They can sign in with their phone number, check their dues, give online and manage their household. Staff, such as the treasurer, secretary and administrators, see extra screens for their job, controlled by the **role** given to each person.

## What the application can do

| Area | What it covers | Main users |
|---|---|---|
| **Membership** | Member and household records, children and dependents, contact details, baptism name, parish join date | Secretary, Admin |
| **Giving** | Membership dues, tithes, offerings, vows, building fund, pledge drive gifts and other donations | Treasurer, finance team |
| **Ways to pay** | Cash and checks entered by the treasurer, online card and bank (ACH) payments through **Stripe**, **Zelle**, the **Square** card reader, and employer giving through **Benevity** | Treasurer |
| **Bank reconciliation** | Uploading the Chase bank statement and matching every line to a recorded payment or expense | Treasurer, Bookkeeper |
| **Expenses** | Checks and cash paid out, payroll, vendors, expense categories (GL codes) | Treasurer, AP team |
| **Pledge drives** | Fundraising campaigns, member pledges, what is paid and what is still owed | Treasurer, Admin, Leadership |
| **Member loans** | Money members lent to the church. It is a debt, not a donation | Treasurer |
| **Reports** | Weekly collection, payment and dues reports, expense report, board summary, member directory, tax statements | Treasurer, Leadership |
| **Communication** | SMS (text messages) to members, departments and pledge donors; public announcements; voicemail inbox | Secretary, Relationship department |
| **Church life** | Departments and ministries, meetings and tasks, photo gallery, calendar, live stream, bylaws | Everyone |

The application is **bilingual**. Every page can be shown in English or Tigrigna (ትግርኛ) using the language switch in the top menu.

## Who uses it

- **Members** sign in to see their own profile, household, dues and giving history, and to give online.
- **The treasurer and finance team** record payments and expenses, reconcile the bank, manage pledges and print reports. This is the most detailed part of the guide.
- **The secretary and administrators** keep member records accurate and decide who has which role.
- **The relationship department** welcomes new members and posts announcements.
- **Church leadership** reads reports and decides who is responsible for what.
- **A technical administrator** keeps the website, server, database and connected services running.

## Words used in this guide

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Transaction** or **payment** | One recorded amount of money received, for example a $50 tithe. Every payment also creates a matching **ledger entry**, which is the accounting record. |
| **Ledger** | The church’s book of accounts: every amount in and out, with its category. |
| **GL code** | “General ledger” code, the accounting category, such as INC001 Membership or INC004 Special Donation. |
| **Reconciliation** | Checking that what the application recorded matches what actually arrived in the bank. |
| **Household** | A head of household plus their spouse and dependents. Dues are worked out per household. |
| **Pledge** | A promise to give an amount during a pledge drive (fundraising campaign). |
| **Role** | What a person is allowed to see and do, such as Treasurer, Secretary or Member. |

There is a longer [glossary](#/help/glossary) in the help section.
