This page is the treasurer’s routine on one page. Each item links to step-by-step instructions.

:::tip Before you start
Ask the Admin to give you the **Treasurer** role. See [Roles](#/administration/roles-and-permissions). Then sign in and confirm you see **Treasurer** under *Church administration* on your Dashboard.
:::

## Every Sunday (or the day collections are counted)

:::steps
1. **Record every cash and check gift** with **Treasurer → Add Payment**, using the receipt number from the paper receipt book. See [Cash and checks](#/donations/cash-and-check).
2. **Record Square card-reader sales and gifts** from **Square Review** once the day’s payments appear. See [Square](#/donations/square).
3. **Print the Weekly Collection Report** for the counting team to sign, and use its **Net to Deposit** figure for the bank deposit. See [Weekly Collection Report](#/reports/weekly-collection-report).
4. **Deposit the cash and checks** at the bank on Monday to Wednesday, so the Sunday Deposit Check can confirm them.
:::

## Every week

:::steps
1. **Zelle Review**: match each new Zelle email to a member and create the payment. See [Zelle](#/donations/zelle).
2. **Download the Chase activity CSV** and upload it on **Bank Integration**. See [Uploading the bank statement](#/reconciliation/uploading-the-bank-statement).
3. **Work through pending bank rows**: approve, link, or record as expenses. See [Reviewing bank transactions](#/reconciliation/reviewing-bank-transactions).
4. **Check the Sunday Deposit Check** bar on the Overview tab. Investigate any Sunday that does not match. See [Sunday Deposit Check](#/dashboard/sunday-deposit-check).
5. **Record expenses**: every check written and every cash payment. See [Recording expenses](#/expenses/recording-expenses).
:::

## Every month

:::steps
1. Open **Bank Integration → Monthly Summary** and confirm the month shows **Reconciled** (no pending rows).
2. On **Overview**, check that the ledger matches the bank. A red “Doesn’t match the bank statement” message means work remains. See [Financial Overview](#/dashboard/financial-overview).
3. Check **Skipped receipts** (Member Payments) and **Skipped checks** (Expenses). Explain or void each gap. See [Receipt numbers](#/donations/cash-and-check?h=receipt-numbers-and-the-skipped-receipt-check).
4. Run the **Expense Report** and resolve anything under **Uncategorized / Needs Review**.
5. Confirm the **Ledger Backup** ran recently. See [Ledger backup](#/reports/ledger-backup).
:::

## Pledge drives (during a drive)

- Record pledges as they come in (**Pledges → Record Pledge**), and check that payments are credited to them.
- Use the **Pledge Dashboard** to report progress to leadership. See [Pledge dashboard](#/pledges/pledge-dashboard).

## Every year

:::steps
1. **January**: members can download their **Annual Contribution Statement** for the year just ended from their Dues page. Be ready to print statements for members who ask. See [Tax statements](#/reports/tax-statements).
2. **Import Benevity** reports as they arrive during the year, and certainly before statements are issued. See [Benevity](#/donations/benevity).
3. **Board report**: print the **Board Summary** for the year. See [Board Summary](#/reports/board-summary).
4. **Hand-over**: if you are stepping down, walk your successor through this page, then ask the Admin to move the Treasurer role.
:::

:::warning The golden rule
**Every dollar is recorded once.** Most problems in the history of this application came from the same money being recorded twice: from the Zelle email *and* the bank statement, or from two copies of the same bank line. Before you create a payment, ask “has this already been recorded another way?” The screens help you: read every “possible duplicate” warning before clicking past it.
:::
