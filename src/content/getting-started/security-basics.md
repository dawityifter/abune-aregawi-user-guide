The application holds members’ names, phone numbers, addresses, baptism names, family details and every gift they have made. Treat it with the same care as the church’s offering box and paper registers.

## Every staff user

:::steps
1. **Keep your phone locked.** Signing in uses text-message codes, so anyone holding your unlocked phone can sign in as you.
2. **Never share a sign-in code.** No one from the church will ever ask you for the code. Anyone asking is trying to get into your account.
3. **Sign out on shared computers.** Use **Sign Out** when you finish, and close the browser.
4. **Don’t download member lists unless you need them.** Printed or saved reports, such as the household directory, contain personal information. Store them safely and shred or delete them after use.
5. **Don’t send member information by text, WhatsApp or personal email.** Share inside the application, or by the church’s official email.
6. **Report anything odd at once.** For example, a role you didn’t ask for, a payment you didn’t record, or a text code you didn’t request. Tell the technical administrator and the treasurer.
:::

## Treasurers and finance staff

- Bank statements (Chase CSV files) and Benevity reports contain donors’ names and sometimes addresses. **Delete them from your Downloads folder after uploading.**
- Never email a CSV export to a personal address.
- Before printing the **Board Summary** for a wider audience, use **Wider audience version**. It hides open items that can name individuals.
- If you are handing over the treasurer role, ask the Admin to remove your role on your last day.

## Administrators

- Have **two Admins**, never one, and no more than necessary.
- Review roles at least once a year. See [Roles — who can do what](#/getting-started/roles-and-permissions).
- Check **Activity Logs** if you suspect a change was made without permission.
- Passwords and keys for the church’s outside accounts (Stripe, Square, GitHub, hosting, and so on) belong in the **church’s password manager**, shared by at least two trusted people. They never go in this guide, in email, or on paper left in the office. See [the integration inventory](#/continuity/integrations).
