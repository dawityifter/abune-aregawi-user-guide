**Treasurer → Member Dues** shows everything about one household’s giving for a year. Use it to answer a member’s question (“Am I up to date?”), check a correction, or print a statement.

## Opening a member’s record

:::steps
1. **Treasurer → Member Dues**.
2. Search for the member by name or phone and select them.
3. The **Household Finances** view opens: “{name}’s Household”. Choose the **Year** at the top.
:::

## What it shows

| Panel | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Membership Dues** | **Annual Pledge**, **Monthly Value**, **Paid To Date**, **Balance Due**, and **% COMPLETE**. Dues are calculated from the member’s parish join date. |
| **Total Associated** | Everything connected to the household this year: “{dues} dues + {additional} additional + {match} employer match”, with **Personal giving** shown separately |
| **Additional Contributions Breakdown** | Pledge Drive, Building Fund, Tithes, Donations, Offerings, Vows, Fundraisers, Other |
| **Annual Distribution Timeline** | Month by month: **Paid**, **Pending**, **Upcoming** |
| **Detailed Transaction Ledger ({year})** | Every payment: **Post Date**, **Reference**, **Type**, **Mechanism** (method), **Origin**, **Amount** |

:::note Carried-over and prepaid dues
If the member paid more than a year’s dues, the extra is carried forward (“{amount} paid beyond {year} dues is carried to {next}”). Payments made in another year but counted toward this year are listed separately (“Paid in another year, counted toward {year} dues”).
:::

:::note Employer matches are shown here but are not personal giving
Benevity employer matches appear in **Total Associated** and in the ledger, but **not** in “Personal giving” and never on the tax statement. See [Benevity](#/donations/benevity).
:::

:::note The ledger also includes purchases and loans
Religious item purchases, merchandise and member loans appear in the ledger list, but **not** in the dues or giving totals.
:::

## Adding a payment from here

Click **Add Transaction**. The Add Payment form opens with this member already chosen. See [Cash and checks](#/donations/cash-and-check).

## Printing a statement

Click **Print Statement** (it shows “Generating...”). A PDF **Annual Contribution Statement** for the household and selected year is produced. Treasurers can print one for any member and any year. See [Tax statements](#/reports/tax-statements).
