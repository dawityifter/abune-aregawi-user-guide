## How a household works

- The **head of household** is a full member record with a phone number. Dues are pledged by the household.
- **Dependents** (spouse, children, other relatives) are attached to the head of household. A dependent may have their own phone and email, but they do not need one.
- A household shares one **Family ID**. When the Edit Member window says “This member is part of a household (Family ID: …). Payments are aggregated”, it means the household’s payments are added together for dues and statements.
- The **tax statement** is issued to the head of household and covers the whole household.

## Adding a spouse or child

There are two ways.

**Staff (Secretary/Admin):**

:::steps
1. **Admin Panel → Manage Members**, find the head of household.
2. Click **Add Dependent** on their row, or **Edit → Family Info → Add Dependent**.
3. Enter **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Gender** and **Relationship** (required), and optionally date of birth, phone, email, baptism name, and **Is Baptized**.
4. Save. The dependent appears under the head of household.
:::

**The member themself:** the head of household opens **Dashboard → Children & Dependents** (the page is titled **Spouse & Dependents**) and clicks **Add Dependent**. See [What members can do themselves](#/members/member-self-service).

:::note Only the head of household manages the family
A dependent who signs in sees the household record but is told that family updates should be handled by the head of household.
:::

## A dependent who signs in with their own phone

If a dependent has their own phone number on file, they can sign in. If their profile is not yet linked to a head of household, their Dashboard offers **Start Self-Claim**, a short check that links them to the household.

## Promoting a dependent to their own member account

When a child grows up and should have their own membership, for example to pay their own dues or join departments:

:::steps
1. Make sure the dependent record has a **phone number**.
2. **Manage Members → Edit** the head of household → **Family Info**.
3. Click **Promote** next to the dependent and confirm: “Promote … to have their own member account?”
4. The person can now sign in independently.
:::

:::admin
Promoting needs **Admin** or **Church Leadership**.
:::

## Common problems

| Problem | Cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| A spouse registered separately as a second head of household | They didn’t enter the head of household’s phone at registration | Treat as a duplicate: see [Duplicate and incorrect records](#/members/duplicate-and-incorrect-records) |
| A dependent can’t see their dues | Their profile isn’t linked to a head of household | Use **Start Self-Claim**, or ask the Secretary to link them |
| Household dues look too low | A payment was recorded under a family member who is in a different household | Check the payment’s member on the Member Dues tab |
