Members can do a lot without the treasurer or secretary. Point them to these pages first.

## Profile

**Dashboard → Profile**: name, phone, email, date of birth, gender, marital status, address, emergency contact, baptism name, Date Joined Parish, Interested in Serving, and **Language Preference**. Click **Edit Profile**, make changes, then **Save**.

Dependents’ address comes from the head of household (“Address information is inherited from the head of household”).

## Dues

**Dashboard → Dues** shows the household’s **Member Dues** for the chosen year: **Yearly Pledge**, **Collected**, **Balance Due**, **Additional Contributions**, a **Monthly Status** grid, and **Payment History** (date, amount, type, method, receipt number, note, status).

:::note Members cannot pay dues online themselves
Online giving on **Give** is for donations and pledge payments. Membership dues are paid by cash, check or Zelle, or by card or ACH **through the treasurer**, who uses Add Payment. This is deliberate: the treasurer decides which year the dues cover.
:::

## Annual Contribution Statement

On the Dues page, at the bottom: **Annual Contribution Statement** with **Print Statement**. It produces the tax-deductible giving statement for the household for a **past** year. The current year’s statement becomes available after the year ends.

## Give

**Dashboard → Give** (or the **Give** button at the bottom on a phone). See [Online giving with Stripe](#/donations/online-giving-stripe). The page also explains **Other ways to give**, including Zelle with the church’s Zelle QR code and email address.

## Make a Pledge

**Make a Pledge** in the top menu opens the pledge page when a pledge drive is live. If the member already has a pledge, the page shows it and offers **Pay now** or **Give again**. If no drive is running: “No fundraising drive is running right now”.

## Children & Dependents

Heads of household manage their spouse and children here. See [Households and dependents](#/members/households-and-dependents).

## Questions members often ask

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| “My payment isn’t showing.” | Cash and checks appear once the treasurer records them, usually the same week. Zelle appears after the treasurer reviews it. Card gifts appear immediately. |
| “My dues say I’m behind but I paid.” | The payment may be recorded under another household member, or under a duplicate record. Ask the treasurer to check on the Member Dues tab. |
| “Why isn’t my employer’s match on my statement?” | The match is the employer’s gift, not the member’s. See [Benevity](#/donations/benevity). |
| “I can’t sign in.” | See [Signing in](#/getting-started/signing-in). |
