Members can do a lot without the treasurer or secretary. Point them to these pages first.

## Profile

**Dashboard → Profile**: name, phone, email, date of birth, gender, marital status, address, emergency contact, baptism name, Date Joined Parish, Interested in Serving, and **Language Preference**. Click **Edit Profile**, make changes, then **Save**.

A member who joined through the site was not asked for gender, marital status or an emergency contact. Until those are filled in here, a **Finish your profile** card on their Dashboard names what is missing. **Complete my profile** opens this page already in edit mode, with the cursor in the first missing item. The emergency contact (name and phone number) has its own **Emergency contact** section, below **Contact & Address**.

Dependents’ address comes from the head of household (“Address information is inherited from the head of household”).

## Dues

**Dashboard → Dues** shows the household’s **Member Dues** for the chosen year: **Yearly Pledge**, **Collected**, **Balance Due**, **Additional Contributions**, a **Monthly Status** grid, and **Payment History** (date, amount, type, method, receipt number, note, status).

### Paying dues online

When a balance is due, **Pay my dues** appears on the Dashboard card and at the top of the Dues page. It opens the Give page already set up for dues:

:::steps
1. The page is headed **Paying your {year} membership dues** and shows **Balance due**. The amount is filled in with the balance; change it if you wish to pay more or less.
2. Pay by card, bank account (ACH), Apple Pay or Google Pay, as for any gift.
3. The payment is recorded as **membership dues for that year**, not as a donation. When it goes through, you are taken back to the **Dues page** for that year with a confirmation (“Thank you! Your $400.00 payment was received and applied to your 2026 dues.”). The payment appears in **Payment History** and in the household’s record like any other dues payment; if it is not listed at first, the page checks again after a few seconds. A bank-account (ACH) payment is listed as **Pending** until the bank confirms it.
:::

:::note Paying more than is owed
Anything paid beyond what is owed for the year is **applied to the following year**. The Dues page shows it as carried forward, and next year’s months start out paid. A member who is already paid up for the year can still use **Pay my dues**: what they pay goes to the next year.
:::

**Pay my dues** is for the signed-in member’s own household. It is not offered to a dependent who is viewing the head of household’s dues, and a visitor who is not signed in can only give a donation. Cash, checks and Zelle are still recorded by the treasurer, as before.

## Annual Contribution Statement

On the Dues page, at the bottom: **Annual Contribution Statement** with **Print Statement**. It produces the tax-deductible giving statement for the household for a **past** year. The current year’s statement becomes available after the year ends.

## Give

**Dashboard → Give**, or **Giving → Give** at the bottom of a phone. See [Online giving with Stripe](#/donations/online-giving-stripe). The page also explains **Other ways to give**, including Zelle with the church’s Zelle QR code and email address.

## Make a Pledge

**Make a Pledge** in the top menu (on a phone, in **More** and on the **Giving** page) opens the pledge page when a pledge drive is live. If the member already has a pledge, the page shows it and offers **Pay now** or **Give again**. If no drive is running: “No fundraising drive is running right now”.

## Children & Dependents

Heads of household manage their spouse and children here. See [Households and dependents](#/members/households-and-dependents). Removing someone asks **Are you sure you want to delete this dependent?** on the page itself, and a failed save shows its message at the top of the page.

## Ministries and requests to join

**Dashboard → My Service** lists the ministries a member belongs to. On **Browse All**, **Request to Join** sends a request to the church staff, who read it in **Messages → Volunteer requests**, and the button then reads **Requested**. See [Voicemail and volunteers](#/administration/voicemail-and-volunteers).

## Parish events

**This week at the parish** on the Dashboard, and **Upcoming at the parish** on the **Calendar** page, list events staff have published. **Add to my calendar** downloads a file the phone opens in its own calendar app.

## Questions members often ask

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| “My payment isn’t showing.” | Cash and checks appear once the treasurer records them, usually the same week. Zelle appears after the treasurer reviews it. Card gifts appear immediately. |
| “My dues say I’m behind but I paid.” | The payment may be recorded under another household member, or under a duplicate record. Ask the treasurer to check on the Member Dues tab. |
| “Why isn’t my employer’s match on my statement?” | The match is the employer’s gift, not the member’s. See [Benevity](#/donations/benevity). |
| “I can’t sign in.” | See [Signing in](#/getting-started/signing-in). |
