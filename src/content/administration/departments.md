**Admin Panel → Departments** (“Departments & Ministries — Manage church departments and ministry groups”).

:::admin
- **View:** Admin, Church Leadership, Secretary, Treasurer
- **Create and edit:** Admin, Church Leadership, Secretary
- **Delete:** Admin
:::

## Creating a department

:::steps
1. Click **Create Department**.
2. Enter **Department Name**, **Description** and **Type**: Ministry, Committee, Service, Social or Administrative.
3. Optional: **Parent Department** (or **None (Top Level)**), **Leader** (search by name, ID or phone), **Meeting Schedule**, **Contact Info** (public email and phone), **Max Members**, and **Public Department**.
4. Click **Create**.
:::

The list shows **Total Departments**, **Members Enrolled** and counts **By Type**, with search and type filters.

## Department members

Open a department and use **Manage Members** to add or remove people. Only **active departments with members** can be sent group SMS.

## Meetings and tasks

Members of a department see it under **Dashboard → My Service** (“Departments & Service”). On the department’s page, leaders can:

- **Add Meeting**: date, agenda, attendance and minutes. Open a meeting for its details, edit it, and **email** the minutes to members.
- **Add Task**: a task with a status and priority, assigned to someone.

:::note Joining a department
Members can browse departments, but the **Request to Join** button says “Request to join functionality coming soon!”. Today, a leader or the secretary adds people.
:::
