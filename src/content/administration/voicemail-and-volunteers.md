**Admin Panel → Messages**.

:::admin
The voicemail inbox loads for **Admin**, **Church Leadership** and **Secretary**.
:::

## Voicemail Inbox

Calls to the church’s **Twilio** phone number can leave a voicemail. Each one appears with **Date**, **From**, **Duration**, the **Recording** (play it in the browser) and a **Transcription** (or “No transcription available” / “Pending...”).

- **Delete voicemail** removes it after you have dealt with it.
- Use **Refresh** to check for new ones.

## Volunteer Requests

“Members interested in serving” lists people who said they would like to volunteer, with any message and whether they asked for a call (**Call Requested**). After contacting someone, mark the request **Responded**. It is then hidden from the list.

:::tip Make someone responsible
Voicemails and volunteer requests are only useful if someone checks them. Assign a person (usually the Secretary) to check **Messages** weekly.
:::
