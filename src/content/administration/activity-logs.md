**Admin Panel → Activity Logs** (Admin and Church Leadership).

## Activity Logs

Every important change is recorded: member edits, role changes, payments created or edited, SMS sent, Benevity imports and more. Filter by action (**CREATE**, **UPDATE**, **DELETE**, **POST**, **PUT**) and type (**Member**, **Donation**, **Payment**), then **Refresh**. Each entry shows **Date**, **User**, **Action**, **Entity** and **Details**. “System / Unknown” means an automatic process.

Use it to answer questions like “who changed this member’s phone number?” or “when was this payment edited?”.

## Website Analytics

At the top of the tab, **Website Analytics** links to the church’s own analytics dashboard (**Open analytics dashboard**): page views, referrers and traffic for the website. It runs on the church’s own server, uses no cookies, and identifies no individual members.
