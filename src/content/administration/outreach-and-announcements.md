**Dashboard → Relationship Department** opens **Outreach & Member Relations**.

:::admin
**Admin** and **Relationship** only.
:::

## Welcoming new members

- **Pending Welcomes** lists “Members who registered but haven’t been welcomed yet.”
- After you have contacted someone, click **Mark Welcomed** and add a **Welcome Note**, for example “Called; family of four; interested in Sunday school”.
- **Already Welcomed** shows Member ID, Family Size, Date Joined, **Welcomed By** and the note.

“All caught up! No pending welcomes.” means nobody is waiting.

## Public announcements

Under **Public Announcements**, create, edit or cancel announcements that appear on the church website’s home page (Parish Announcements). Write them in both English and Tigrigna where the form allows.

## Church TV View

**Church TV View** shows announcements full-screen, rotating, for a television in the church hall. The rotation interval (in seconds) is adjustable on the same page. Open it on the TV’s browser and leave it running.
