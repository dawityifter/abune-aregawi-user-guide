## Principles

1. **Two people for everything critical.** No account, role or duty depends on one person.
2. **Church-owned accounts.** Services are registered to church email addresses, never personal ones.
3. **Separate duties for money.** The person who records money is not the only person who checks it.
4. **Write it down.** Every hand-over includes updating this matrix and the password manager.
