**Treasurer → Reports → Expense Report**: “What the church spent the money on, by month and by category”.

## Choosing what to see

Set **Start Date** and **End Date** (or **Year**), and optionally filter by **Category**, **Payment Method**, **Source**, **Status**, or search a **Payee**. **Clear filters** resets them.

## Sections

| Section | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Summary cards | **Current Month**, **Average Monthly**, **Largest Category**, **Number of Expenses** (recorded expenses plus uncategorized bank debits) |
| **Expenses need categorization** | Bank debits with no category, counted as **Uncategorized / Needs Review**. Click **Review these** and give them a category |
| **Monthly Expense Trend** | Every month in the range, including months with no spending |
| **Monthly Expenses by Category** | Category × month table. **Click any amount** to see the transactions behind it |
| **YTD Expenses by Category** | GL code, year-to-date total and **% of Total** |
| **How this ties to the Monthly Summary** | Compares the report total with bank debits: **Recorded but not on a statement** (e.g. cash), **Returned deposited items (excluded)**, **Timing** (written and cleared in different months), and the **Difference** |

The tie-out only appears without category, payee, method, source or status filters, because bank activity can’t be narrowed the same way.

## Export and print

- **Export CSV** downloads the figures for a spreadsheet.
- **Print** produces a paper or PDF copy headed with the church name, period, **Bank data through** date and filters.

:::note Salary months
Salary is counted in the month the check was written. Two months paid together is normal, so a $0 month next to a double month is not an exception. Compare salary by quarter or year to date.
:::
