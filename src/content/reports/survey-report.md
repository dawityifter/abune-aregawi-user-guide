Members and visitors can answer the **Church Services Survey** at the `/survey` page. It works in English or Tigrigna, and unfinished answers are saved on the device so people can come back to them.

**Admin Panel → Survey Report** (“Church Services Survey — Report”) shows:

- **Total Responses**
- **Member Status** of respondents
- Each question’s answers, with “{answered} of {total} answered”
- **Survey Language** used
- **Free-text answers**

“No responses yet.” means nobody has submitted the survey.

:::note Privacy
Free-text answers can contain names or personal details that respondents typed in. Read them before sharing the report beyond leadership, and remove anything personal.
:::
