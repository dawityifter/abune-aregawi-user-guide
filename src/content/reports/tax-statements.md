The **Annual Contribution Statement** lists a household’s tax-deductible gifts for one calendar year, as a PDF with the church’s logo. It says no goods or services were provided in return, and asks the member to keep it for their tax records.

## Who can print one

| Who | Where | Which years |
|---|---|---|
| The member (head of household) | **Dues** page → **Annual Contribution Statement** → **Print Statement** | Past years only. A year’s statement becomes available after it ends |
| Treasurer or Admin | **Treasurer → Member Dues** → choose member and year → **Print Statement** | Any year |

## What is included

A gift is on the statement when its type is a deductible gift and its **payment date** is in that year:

| Included | Not included |
|---|---|
| Membership dues, tithes, offerings, vows, building fund, donations, pledge drive gifts, Tigray Hunger Fundraiser, event gifts | **Employer matches** (Benevity), never |
| | Religious item sales and event merchandise (purchases) |
| | Member loans and repayments |
| | Failed or refunded payments, *if they have been marked as such* |

The statement covers the whole **household** (the head of household and linked family members).

:::note The date that counts is the payment date
A gift counts in the year it was **paid**. Dues paid in December for the next year are on **this** year’s statement, even though they count toward next year’s dues. That follows the usual rule that a cash gift is deductible in the year it is given.
:::

:::warning Make sure the year’s books are complete before statements go out
Before January statement requests:
1. Import every **Benevity** report for the year.
2. Clear Zelle Review and the year’s pending bank lines, especially late-December gifts.
3. Have any **refunded** or **bounced** payments corrected. They are not removed automatically.
4. Check that records with **Missing address** are completed (Manage Members filter).
:::

:::confirm Open tax questions for the church’s advisor
- Benevity may send its own receipts to donors. Should the church statement also list Benevity personal gifts?
- Whether a particular past **religious item sale** that appears on a statement should be there is awaiting a decision.
- The church’s EIN printed on statements comes from the server configuration (`CHURCH_EIN`). Check it on a printed statement once a year.
:::

:::note Emailing statements
The server can email a statement, but there is no button for it on screen. Print to PDF and send it from the church’s email if a member asks.
:::
