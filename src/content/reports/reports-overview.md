| Question | Report | Where | Main reader |
|---|---|---|---|
| What did we collect this week, and what should go to the bank? | [Weekly Collection Report](#/reports/weekly-collection-report) | Treasurer → Reports | Treasurer, counting team |
| Who is up to date with dues, and who is behind? | [Payment Reports](#/reports/payment-reports) | Treasurer → Reports | Treasurer, Leadership |
| How much did we take in and spend this year, and does it match the bank? | [Financial Overview](#/dashboard/financial-overview) | Treasurer → Overview | Treasurer, Leadership |
| What did we spend money on, by month and category? | [Expense Report](#/reports/expense-report) | Treasurer → Reports | Treasurer, Budget Committee |
| One printable financial summary for the board | [Board Summary](#/reports/board-summary) | Treasurer → Reports | Board / Leadership |
| How is the pledge drive going? | [Pledge Dashboard](#/pledges/pledge-dashboard) | Treasurer → Pledges | Leadership |
| Monthly income, expense and balance from the bank | Monthly Summary | Treasurer → Bank Integration | Treasurer |
| A member’s giving for a year, or their tax statement | [Member financial record](#/members/member-financial-record), [Tax statements](#/reports/tax-statements) | Treasurer → Member Dues | Treasurer, member |
| Membership numbers, household directory | [Member reports](#/reports/member-reports) | Admin Panel → Reports | Admin, Secretary |
| Results of the church services survey | [Survey report](#/reports/survey-report) | Admin Panel → Survey Report | Leadership |
| A spreadsheet copy of the whole ledger | [Ledger backup](#/reports/ledger-backup) | Treasurer → Ledger Backup | Treasurer, auditor |

:::tip Before any report goes to leadership
1. Upload the latest bank statement and clear pending lines.
2. Check the Overview’s ledger-versus-bank message.
3. Note the **Bank data through** date that the report prints. A report is only as current as the last bank upload.
:::

## Printing and exporting

- Most reports have a **Print** button, which opens the browser’s print dialog. Choose **Save as PDF** to keep a file.
- The **Expense Report** also has **Export CSV**, for spreadsheets.
- The Member Information report offers **Save as PDF**.
- Printed reports contain names and amounts. Store them safely.
