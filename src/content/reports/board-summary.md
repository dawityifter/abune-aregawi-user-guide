**Treasurer → Reports → Board Summary**: “A printable summary of income, spending and cash for the board”.

## Producing it

:::steps
1. Choose the **Year** and **Through month**.
2. Read the first page’s **To resolve before this is final** list. Settle each item, then reload.
3. If the copy goes beyond the board, tick **Wider audience version**.
4. Optionally edit the **What to take away** points. “You can edit this text before printing. Edits are not saved.”
5. Click **Print**.
:::

## Pages

| Page | Contents |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | **Income**, **Spending**, **Income minus spending** (“More came in than went out” or the reverse), **Day-to-day** spending per month, **Cash in bank** as of a date with the change, a **Spending by month** chart split into day-to-day and building projects, **Where the money went**, and **What to take away** |
| **Monthly detail** | Spending by category for each month, and year to date |
| **Tie-out to the bank** | **Opening bank balance** + **Income** + **Loans received, less repayments** − **Spending** ± timing items = **Expected closing balance**, compared with the **Actual closing balance**. Shows any **Unexplained difference**, and lists checks outstanding over 90 days and repeated charges |

## The “To resolve” list

Before the report is final, it lists data problems that make the figures unreliable:

- “{n}% of spending has a category. {n} expenses ({amount}) still need one.”
- “{n} possible duplicates ({amount}) may be counted twice.”
- “{n} repeated charges ({amount} extra) to check with the payee.”
- “{n} checks outstanding for over 90 days ({amount}).”
- “Unexplained difference in cash: {amount}.”

When everything is settled: “The records are clear. Nothing is waiting to be resolved.”

:::warning Wider audience version
It hides the list of open items. If anything is unresolved it warns: “This wider audience version hides the list of open items, so settle them before sharing this copy.” With few staff, a monthly personnel figure can reveal one person’s pay, so consider this before sharing widely.
:::
