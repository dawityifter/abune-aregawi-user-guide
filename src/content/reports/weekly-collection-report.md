**Treasurer → Reports → 📅 Weekly Collection Report.**

A week runs **Monday to Sunday**. Use **Previous Week** and **Next Week** to move between weeks.

## What it shows

- Totals by payment method: **Cash**, **Check**, **Zelle**, **Credit Card**, **Debit Card**, **ACH**, **Other**.
- For each method: **Total Income**, the expenses paid by that method, and **Net to Deposit** (income minus expenses).
- **💰 Net Deposit**: what should be deposited in the bank. For the Sunday deposit, the **Cash** and **Check** lines are what matter. Zelle, card and ACH are already in the bank.
- **Show** / **Hide** **Details** lists every transaction: **Date**, **Type**, **Member**, **Amount**, **Category**, **Payee**, **Check #**.

## Using it on Sunday

:::steps
1. Record every cash and check gift with Add Payment first.
2. Open the report for the current week and click **Print**.
3. The counters compare the printed **Cash** and **Check** totals with the money counted, and sign the printout.
4. Deposit that amount. The [Sunday Deposit Check](#/dashboard/sunday-deposit-check) later confirms the bank received it.
:::

:::tip If the printout and the counted money disagree
Look at **Details** for a mistyped amount, a payment entered under the wrong method (Check instead of Cash), or a gift dated the wrong week. Correct it before depositing. See [Correcting a payment](#/donations/correcting-a-payment).
:::
