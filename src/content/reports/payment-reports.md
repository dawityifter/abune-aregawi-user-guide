**Treasurer → Reports → Payment Reports.**

:::steps
1. Choose the **Report Type**: **Summary Report**, **Behind on Payments**, or **Monthly Breakdown**.
2. Click **Generate Report**.
3. Click **Print** for a paper or PDF copy.
:::

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Summary Report** | **Total Members**, **Up to Date**, **Behind**, **Collection Rate**, **Total Due**, **Total Collected** |
| **Behind on Payments** | **Members Behind on Payments**: member, contact, amount collected and balance owed |
| **Monthly Breakdown** | **Monthly Collection Report**: collections by month, including the Tigray Hunger Fundraiser |

:::warning Handle the “Behind” list with care
It lists members by name with what they owe. Share it only with the people who follow up, never on a notice board or in a group chat. To send polite reminders, use SMS to individuals rather than circulating the list.
:::

:::note Why a member can look “behind” wrongly
- Their payment is recorded under another household member who isn’t linked to their household.
- They are registered twice (see [Duplicate records](#/members/duplicate-and-incorrect-records)).
- Their **Yearly Pledge** or **Date Joined Parish** is wrong.
- They pay twice a year. Behind is measured against what is expected *by this month*.
:::
