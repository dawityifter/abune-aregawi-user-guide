Sometimes a member **lends** money to the church, for example toward a building payment, expecting to be repaid. That is a **debt the church owes**, not a gift. The **Loans (Not Donations)** tab keeps these separate:

> “These are liability records — loans from members. They are NOT donations and NOT tax-deductible.”

:::admin
Only **Treasurer** and **Admin** can record loans and repayments.
:::

## Recording a loan

:::steps
1. **Treasurer → Loans (Not Donations) → Record Loan**.
2. In **Record Member Loan**, search for the **Member**.
3. Enter the **Loan Amount**, **Payment Method** (Cash, Check, Zelle, Other), **Loan Date** and **Notes**, for example the agreed repayment terms.
4. Save. The loan appears with status **Active**.
:::

## Recording a repayment

:::steps
1. Find the loan and click **Repayment**.
2. The window shows the **Original Amount** and **Outstanding Balance**.
3. Enter the **Repayment Amount**, **Payment Method**, **Repayment Date** and **Notes**.
4. Save. When the balance reaches zero, the loan becomes **Closed**. Until then it counts as **Partially Repaid**.
:::

The top of the tab shows **Total Outstanding**, **Active Loans**, **Partially Repaid** and **Total Loaned**. Filter by status and dates to find a loan.

## How loans affect the numbers

- Loans received are **not income**, and repayments are **not expenses**. The Financial Overview shows them on their own lines: “Loans received in {year} — Not income” and “Loans repaid in {year} — Not an expense”.
- The **Bank Balance** includes borrowed money: “Includes money the church owes, such as member loans”. A high balance during a loan doesn’t mean the money is free to spend.
- Loans never appear on a member’s tax statement or in their giving totals. They do appear in their financial ledger, which notes “This list also includes purchases and loans, which are not in the totals above.”

:::warning Don’t record a loan as a payment
Loan money must not be entered through **Add Payment**, **Zelle Review** or bank **Approve**. Those screens deliberately don’t offer loan types. A loan entered as a donation would put a debt on someone’s tax statement.
:::

:::confirm The bank line for a loan
When the loan money arrives in the bank, for example by Zelle, its bank line still has to be cleared on **Bank Integration**. Because loans aren’t offered there, the line is presumably cleared with **Mark Reconciled** after the loan is recorded on this tab. Confirm the established practice with the current treasurer and write it here.
:::
